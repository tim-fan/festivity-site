💚 Where does the money go?

This is a GoFundMe charity fundraiser: donations go straight to Rady Children’s Hospital Foundation, not to me. You’ll get a receipt by email. I do the mapping for free as a local volunteer, and you can see last year’s Mira Mesa map.

💬 Good to know

Donated anonymously? Your name is hidden from the public, but the organizer can still see it, so your vote still counts. Votes without a matching donation aren’t counted.
